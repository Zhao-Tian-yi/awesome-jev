## Important caveat

TypeSafe has **not** publicly released:
- Jev model weights;
- the full Jev architecture;
- the original training dataset;
- the exact RLCD algorithm/reward;
- the complete training code.

Accordingly, open-source projects listed in this repository are independent implementations or approximations unless explicitly stated otherwise.
